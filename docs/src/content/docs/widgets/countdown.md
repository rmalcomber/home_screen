Give it a **label** and a **date**. It shows the number of days until that date ("25 days until Release 4.2 ships"), or since it ("3 days since …") once it has passed, and updates through the day.

## Several countdowns in one tile

Choose **+ Add countdown** to keep more than one date in the same tile, like the [clock](/widgets/clock/) and [weather](/widgets/weather/) widgets. Use the ▲ and ▼ buttons to reorder them, or tick **Sort by date, soonest first** to let the tile order them for you (upcoming dates first, then past ones, newest first).

With more than one, the tile shows a **List** (label and date on the left, the day count on the right) or **Mini tiles** that wrap to fit a narrow tile. Choose the layout in the tile's settings.

A countdown saved by an earlier version keeps working: it opens as the first entry.

Use it for releases, holidays, deadlines and anniversaries.
